## Sources studied (2026-07-19)

1. **MobileWan** (Qualcomm, arXiv 2607.06173): Wan2.2-5B on Snapdragon 8 Gen 5 NPU; 5s 480×832@16fps in 20s (0.25× RT on-phone), VBench 83.79. Methods: (a) **recurrence distillation** — chunk-local softmax + causal *linear* attention over past compressed to fixed RNN state (s_t, z_t); O(N) compute, O(1) memory; 30/30 blocks converted drops self-attn 43.3→26.9 TFLOPs at ~0 quality loss; (b) **learnable head pruning** — per-head sigmoid gates + sparsity reg + temperature anneal, gates learned under **high-noise-biased** timesteps (σ~LogitNormal(1.5,1)); 23% pruned free; (c) DMD2 3-step (1-step collapses); (d) memory-optimized VAE decoder. Meta-lesson: don't shrink the model — restructure its execution to fit the hardware envelope.
2. **How to Scale Your Model** (DeepMind scaling book): rooflines (T_math vs T_comms, arithmetic intensity, B_crit ≈ 295 on H100 bf16 = 989e12/3.35e12); prefill compute-bound vs decode BW-bound; KV cache memory math; latency floor = (batch×KV + params)/BW; TP-for-latency past the ICI-efficiency point; predict → measure → close the gap discipline.
3. **TwelveLabs Pegasus 1.5 API**: official `pegasus1.5` synchronous structured video analysis is integrated behind a strict local evidence boundary. Three exact-byte canaries proved transport and parser behavior but materially over-scored motion, anatomy, and static-action failures versus Gemini on the same files; readiness remains `calibration-failed`. See `brain/research/Pegasus-1.5-Video-Judge-Calibration.md`.
4. **Google Gemini video understanding / structured output**: Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview accepted the same uploaded MP4 bytes with explicit `videoMetadata.fps = 16`, strict JSON output, deterministic local re-parsing, and hash-bound scrubbed evidence. Six exact-byte calls were internally repeatable (core-dimension MAE 0.40; 100% within one point), but a nine-video blinded human panel remains mandatory before `quality-qualified`. See `brain/research/Gemini-3.1-Video-Judge-Calibration.md`.

## Technical model (v2 — consensus-corrected)

- 480×832 Wan-class: 8×8 VAE + 2×2 patchify → **1,560 tokens/latent frame**; 1 latent frame = 4 video frames (causal VAE: first latent = 1 frame — footnote the off-by-one); 21 latent frames ≈ 81 frames ≈ 5s@16fps; chunk = 3 latent frames = **4,680 tokens**.
- Real-time budget: one 3-latent-frame chunk per 750ms all-in.
- **Regime**: streaming causal video DiT = a sequence of **small prefills** (4,680 tok ≫ B_crit≈300) → **compute-bound** *in the bounded-context regime* (chunk-local / windowed / linear-state attention). Playbook: steps > attention structure > fp8 compute > kernels/MFU > VAE overlap. KV is a **capacity/long-horizon** concern (287MB/latent frame @1.3B; ~27GB @14B for 21f), not a step-time bandwidth concern (~2ms to read 6GB vs 200-360ms compute).
- Attention FLOPs/step **by policy** (1.3B, 30L, d=1536, chunk 4,680): chunk-local ≈ **4 T**; sliding-window(21f) ≈ **10-28 T**; linear-state past ≈ ~0 + state update (BW-bound, small). Cross-attn to ~512 text tokens ≈ 0.4 T. Linears ≈ 2·P·T ≈ **12.2 T**.
- **Honest fps math** (corrected and measured in the archived development run): 4-step chunk ≈ 90-160 TFLOPs → at **30-40% MFU** → 1.3B single H100 ≈ 22-35 fps DiT-only. Archived CF++ 1-step + streaming TAEHV reports **31.04 fps warm e2e**; 2-step reports **22.16**. Step count dominates; the quality-qualified result does not follow automatically from the fastest row.
- **14B does NOT close on H100** (≈131 TFLOPs/step linears → ~9 fps bf16). 14B real-time is a B200+fp8+1-2-step story only. The measured headline substrate is Wan-1.3B on H100.
- **VAE is a first-class bottleneck** (APT1: VAE > DiT), now directly measured: full Wan VAE gives 19.71/15.76 fps e2e for 1/2-step; batch TAEHV gives 34.37/24.05; corrected rolling streaming gives 31.04/22.16. Separate-stream overlap itself adds only **0.38-0.40%** over serial rolling decode. TAEHV is load-bearing; overlap is not the main win.
- Claim hygiene: no "lossless fp8" (QAT ≠ deployment-quantizer equivalence — must be demonstrated); no "3-step improves quality" without noise caveats (VBench deltas ~1pt are within noise); state MFU assumptions on every prediction.
- Local hardware: Titan Xp (Pascal, fp16 1/64 rate, no FA) = roofline case-study row only. M4 48GB = dev box (1-4 fps by the math; MLX port CUT as a deliverable). Cloud H100/4090 rentals are the workhorse.
